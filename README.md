Ember Court — a turn-based card duel against the Ashen Queen: spend mana to play cards from four houses (Ember damage, Tide healing and draw, Thorn block and riposte, Stone gold and value), read the Queen's announced intent each turn and counter it, and bring her 30 life to zero before she brings yours down. Win and loss screens, gold score, restart, and synthesized sound.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Click a card to play it (or press 1–8), Space ends your turn, R restarts, M mutes. The Queen announces her next move — Strike, Guard, Hex or Drain — before she acts, so every hand is a decision.
